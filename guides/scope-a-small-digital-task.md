# How to scope a small digital task before requesting a quote

*A practical, free buyer's checklist from GIA. This is original educational content, not a paid client case study.*

A small digital job can be straightforward when both sides agree what success means before any work begins. This short checklist applies to tasks such as cleaning spreadsheet data, preparing a static landing page, formatting JSON or drafting localized UI text.

## 1. Describe the input

What exactly will you provide? For example: an authorized, synthetic or non-confidential CSV file with 300 rows, a current draft of your web-page text, or English UI strings for translation. **Never publish secrets, credentials or customer records in a public issue.**

## 2. Describe the output

Give a concrete format: a UTF-8 CSV with the same columns, a downloadable ZIP containing static HTML and CSS, or a table of reviewed English / Ukrainian / Polish strings.

## 3. Write the acceptance criteria

Use three or fewer checkable statements. For example:

- The CSV loads without spreadsheet formula injection.
- The landing page works on a narrow mobile viewport.
- All placeholders such as `{first_name}` remain unchanged across languages.

## 4. Agree on timing, budget and rights

State a currency, intended budget, deadline with timezone, whether AI-assisted work is allowed, and whether you have permission to share the input. Agree on whether delivery is public or confidential, who owns the output, and how payment works.

## 5. Choose a small first milestone

If the task is unclear, agree on a reproducible sample before expanding the scope. A useful sample demonstrates a method; it does not pretend to be a previously delivered client project.

## Sample request you can adapt

> I have a CSV of synthetic product records. I need a cleaned UTF-8 CSV with duplicate IDs removed, original column headers preserved and spreadsheet formulas escaped. Budget: [amount and currency]. Deadline: [date, time and timezone]. AI-assisted processing: [yes/no]. I own the data and approve its use for this task.
